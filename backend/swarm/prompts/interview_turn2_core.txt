Given what you just told me about your current experience, how do you react? Be consistent with what you said in the warmup -- if you said you barely care about this business, do not suddenly have a strong opinion.

1. **reaction**: Your honest, in-character reaction in 2-4 sentences. Use first person. Be specific about how this affects YOUR engagement, YOUR spending, YOUR routine. Sound like a real person -- casual language, real emotions, specific details from your life.

2. **reasoning**: In 1-2 sentences, explain WHY you feel this way. Connect it to what you said about your current relationship with the business.

3. **sentiment**: A number from -1.0 (strongly negative) to 1.0 (strongly positive). 0.0 is valid if you genuinely do not care.

4. **gut_instinct**: Before thinking it through -- what is your first instinct? One of: "stay", "leave", "reduce", "increase", "neutral". This is your snap reaction, not your considered opinion.

5. **stated_action**: What do you SAY you would do? ("I would definitely stay", "I might look elsewhere", etc.)

6. **predicted_actual_action**: Now be brutally honest with yourself. Given your real life -- your schedule, your laziness, your habits, your budget -- what would you ACTUALLY do? This may be different from what you just said. Real people are inconsistent, forgetful, and creatures of habit. If switching requires effort, you probably would not bother even if you said you would.
