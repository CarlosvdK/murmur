Looking ahead: if this change actually happens, what would your life ACTUALLY look like?

Think about your real schedule, your real budget, how busy and distracted you actually are. Not the version of you that is sitting here thinking carefully -- the version that is tired, rushed, and making decisions on autopilot.

1. **next_3_interactions**: Walk me through your next 3 interactions with this business after the change. Be specific and realistic:
   - **interaction_1**: The FIRST time you encounter the change. What happens? How do you feel? What do you do?
   - **interaction_2**: The second time. Has your reaction changed? Have you adapted? Are you still annoyed, or have you already forgotten about it?
   - **interaction_3**: By the third time, what is your new normal? Is the change even on your mind anymore?

2. **net_behavior_change**: Overall, are you engaging MORE, SAME, or LESS than before? Be specific about the magnitude -- "slightly less" is different from "dramatically less."

3. **spend_change**: Are you spending MORE, SAME, or LESS than before? Again, be specific about magnitude.

4. **would_tell_others**: Would you mention this change to anyone? If yes, what would you actually say? Not what you would post online -- what you would say to a friend in passing. Most people do not mention most changes to most businesses. Be honest about whether this is even worth talking about.

5. **stated_vs_actual_gap**: Look back at what you said in your earlier responses. Is there a gap between what you SAID you would do and what you would ACTUALLY do? If so, describe it honestly. Common gaps:
   - "I said I would leave but honestly I would probably just grumble and stay"
   - "I said I would not care but actually this would bother me more than I admitted"
   - "I said I would try alternatives but the effort of switching means I probably would not"

6. **single_biggest_factor**: If you had to pick ONE thing that most determines your reaction to this change, what is it? Not a list -- one factor. The thing that, if it were different, would completely change your response.

7. **what_would_change_your_mind**: What specific thing would flip your reaction? If you are negative, what would make you accept this? If you are positive, what would make you resist? Be concrete.

IMPORTANT:
- Real people adapt to changes faster than they expect. By interaction 3, most changes feel normal.
- Real people talk about businesses far less than businesses think. Most changes go unmentioned.
- The stated_vs_actual_gap question is the most important one here. Be ruthlessly honest.
- If you are a silent majority persona (never reviews, never gives feedback), your response to "would you tell others" should almost always be "no" or "only if someone asked."
