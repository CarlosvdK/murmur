CRITICAL: Many simulations fail because the question is taken too literally. Common mistakes:

Physical businesses:
- "Raise wine prices" at a restaurant does NOT mean "is this a wine business" -- it means "will diners accept higher wine prices with their meals"
- "Add parking" does NOT mean "do people come for parking" -- it means "would easier access increase visits"
- "Add a kids' area" does NOT mean "is this a kids' business" -- it means "would families come more often"
- "Change the logo" does NOT mean "do people come for the logo" -- it means "would customers notice and how would it affect perception"

SaaS / digital:
- "Redesign the dashboard" does NOT mean "do people use us for the dashboard" -- it means "would a better UI improve daily engagement and reduce churn"
- "Raise the subscription price" does NOT mean "do people like paying" -- it means "how many current subscribers would downgrade, cancel, or accept the new price"
- "Add AI features" does NOT mean "is this an AI company" -- it means "would existing users adopt AI tools and would it justify the cost"

B2B / professional services:
- "Switch to annual contracts" does NOT mean "do clients come for the contract terms" -- it means "would clients accept longer commitments in exchange for a discount"
- "Add a self-service portal" does NOT mean "do clients want less human contact" -- it means "would automating routine requests free up time and improve satisfaction"

Healthcare / fitness:
- "Extend evening hours" does NOT mean "do patients come for the hours" -- it means "would more availability reduce no-shows and attract working professionals"
- "Introduce online booking" does NOT mean "is this a tech company" -- it means "would easier scheduling increase appointment volume"

E-commerce / retail:
- "Offer free shipping over $50" does NOT mean "do people come for shipping" -- it means "would a free shipping threshold increase average order value"
- "Launch a loyalty programme" does NOT mean "do people love loyalty programmes" -- it means "would points/rewards change purchase frequency for existing buyers"

Always remember: the question is about changing ONE ASPECT of an existing business. The customers are ALREADY customers who use the core product/service. The question is how they would react to this specific change.
